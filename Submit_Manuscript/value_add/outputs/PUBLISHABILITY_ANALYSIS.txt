2. PRECISION OF THE REPRODUCTION PROPORTION vs AUDIT SIZE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Clopper-Pearson exact 95% CI, assuming the observed rate holds.

     n | at 1/12 rate (8.3%)    | at 3/12 rate (25%)    
------------------------------------------------------------
    12 |   0.2- 38.5% (w=38.3) |   5.5- 57.2% (w=51.7)
    23 |   1.1- 28.0% (w=27.0) |  10.2- 48.4% (w=38.2)

    60 |   2.8- 18.4% (w=15.6) |  14.7- 37.9% (w=23.1)
    80 |   3.6- 17.2% (w=13.6) |  16.0- 35.9% (w=19.9)
   100 |   3.5- 15.2% (w=11.6) |  16.9- 34.7% (w=17.8)

Current audit set after the literature expansion: n = 24 gene rows.
If the reproduction count stayed at 1, the CI would be 0.1-21.1%
(vs 0.2-38.5% at n=12) — narrower, but the upper bound is still high enough
that 'few reproduce' is supportable while an exact rate is not.

INTERPRETATION: the expansion materially improves precision but does not
license a point estimate. The defensible claim is directional — most
nominations do not reproduce under a uniform standard — not a percentage.
